# Rights and reused-content policy

**Not legal advice; no guaranteed fair-use outcome.**
- Inventory rights for source video, promotional clips, stills, licensed music/SFX, synthetic voices and font assets.
- Film commentary/review may be transformative but no number of seconds automatically safe. Mirror/crop/speed change, captions or disclaimers do not create legal permission.
- EDL records each excerpt, source/time range, reason it is necessary for critique, rights status (CLEARED|REQUIRES_REVIEW|BLOCKED), license evidence and human review.
- Prefer original graphics, presenter footage, authorized licensed material where an excerpt lacks justification; do not disguise unauthorized footage.
- Separate platform monetization/reused-content policies from copyright clearance; a content claim may happen even with commentary.
- If source terms or license unknown, mark REQUIRES_REVIEW and block publishing package; do not silently relabel as cleared.
- Users decide publication and accept responsibility for rights; ChatGPT never independently grants permissions or issues legal determinations.
- No automated upload in base scope. Local export after a technical render can remain clearly labeled DRAFT / NOT FOR PUBLICATION until rights gate.
